POOLEAN

Pool basketball, ranked.

A private, admin-run tracker for a basketball group.

Terms and RulesPrivacy

Poolean is invite only. The host adds players to the roster, players sign in with their name and a PIN, check in at parties, and the room ranks the table together. Game stats, the rules of pool basketball, and the poker ledger live in the same place.
