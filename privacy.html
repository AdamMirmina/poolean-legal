Poolean is a private, admin-run tracker for a basketball group. This policy explains what it collects and how that data is used.

What we collect

Your Apple account email (to match you to the roster and sign you in), your name as it appears on the roster, the profile photo you choose to set, your party check-ins, and your votes. That's it. No tracking, no ads, no analytics SDKs.

How it's used

Only to run the app: signing you in, showing the rankings and stats, and letting the group check in and vote at parties. Data lives in Firebase (Google) and isn't sold or shared with third parties for their own use. Any service that processes it does so only to provide the app and gives the same protection described here.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays while your account is active. To delete your account and data, or to revoke access, email adammirmina@gmail.com and it's handled within 24 hours.
