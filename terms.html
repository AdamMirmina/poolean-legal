The deal

Poolean is a private, admin-run tracker for a basketball group. You sign in with Apple, check in at parties, vote on the rankings, and can set a profile photo. By using it you agree to these terms and the community rules below.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Profile photos and anything else you add must not be pornographic, hateful, threatening, or harassing. Content that breaks this is removed and the account loses access.

Reporting and blocking

Every player's profile has a report and a block action. Reports reach the admin and are reviewed within 24 hours; confirmed violations are removed and the account can be suspended. Blocking someone hides their photo from you right away.

Your content

You're responsible for the photo you upload. The admin can remove any photo that breaks the rules and can suspend an account.
