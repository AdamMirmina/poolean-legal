The deal

Poolean is a private, admin-run tracker for one basketball group. The host adds you to the roster; you sign in with your name and a PIN, check in at parties, vote on the rankings, comment, and can set a profile photo. By using it you agree to these terms and the community rules below.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Profile photos, comments, and anything else you add must not be pornographic, hateful, threatening, or harassing. Content that breaks this is removed and the account loses access.

Reporting and blocking

Every player's profile has a report and a block action, and every comment can be reported. Reports reach the admin and are reviewed within 24 hours; confirmed violations are removed and the account can be suspended. Blocking someone hides their photo and comments from you right away.

Your content

You're responsible for the photo you upload and the comments you post. The admin can remove any photo or comment that breaks the rules and can suspend an account.

Poker nights

The poker screen is a ledger of what players settled up between themselves after a home game. Nothing is wagered, bought, or paid through the app.

Community rules

  • No objectionable profile photos. Nothing pornographic, hateful, or designed to harass.
  • No harassment or abuse of other players, in a comment or anywhere else you add content.
  • Report or block anyone who crosses the line. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.
  • Break these and your access is removed. Zero tolerance.
